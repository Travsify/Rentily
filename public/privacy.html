Rentilly Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 2026 | Last Updated: September 2026

Rentilly ("we", "our", or "us"), operated by Travsify Global Technologies Ltd, is dedicated to protecting your personal data in full compliance with the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) and Google Play Developer Policies.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly: full name, verified email address, phone number, Bank Verification Number (BVN) / NIN for statutory KYC under Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN) regulations, property listing records, transaction and escrow records, and device push notification tokens.

2. Purpose and Usage

Collected data is used strictly to authenticate user identity, prevent property fraud, process secure escrow transactions, provide automated prepaid utility bills, and send transaction alerts.

3. Data Security & Storage

All sensitive communications are encrypted in transit via TLS 1.3 and at rest via AES-256. Financial actions require biometric authorization or your personal payment PIN.

4. Third-Party Processors

We partner with licensed institutions for payment processing and banking rails: Paystack (Wema Bank), Maplerad Technologies (9PSB), and Flutterwave. Push notifications are delivered via OneSignal.

5. Account & Data Deletion

Users may request complete account closure and personal data deletion at any time by emailing our Data Protection Officer at support@myrentilly.com.
